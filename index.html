Hening Wang

I am a Ph.D. candidate in General Linguistics at the University of Tübingen and a research assistant in the Linguistic Meaning and Bayesian Modeling (LMBayes) project. My research combines behavioral experiments, Bayesian modeling, and computational pragmatics to study causal and argumentative communication.
